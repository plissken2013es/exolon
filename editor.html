LAYERS

    From back to front. Tick to show in the editor, click the name to edit the layer.

    TILES

    Drag to pick a block of tiles. On the map: click to paint, right-click to erase, Alt+click to pick a tile.

    COLLISION

    The red cells are solid: the player walks on them and can't go through. Click to paint, right-click to erase.

    OBJECTS

    Pick a kind of object and click on the map to place it. With SELECT, drag the objects to move them (Shift: by pixels), right-click to delete.
